# Changelog

**Full Health Forever 0.1.2**

Like Full Mana Forever:
- **Unlocked frame**: a thin edge, the name above it and two buttons, **Lock** and **Settings**;
  drag the frame itself. Groups with nothing in your bags show a grey placeholder, so you see
  where their icon goes. The frame keeps its top left corner where you dropped it.
- **Settings window**: the version in the title, the profile at the top, the language and the
  buttons in a row at the bottom; *Unlock frame (test mode)* instead of *Lock frame*.

Fixed:
- Out of combat only a bandage may light up; a potion, healthstone or defensive no longer lights
  up after a fight (with *Show only in combat* switched off).
- A bandage someone else put on you also counts for the 60 seconds.

**Full Health Forever 0.1.1**

- In the addon menu at the minimap: left-click opens the settings, right-click lets you move
  the frame (a second right-click locks it again).

**Full Health Forever 0.1.0**

The first version.

- **Health bar** with your heals in flight and shields drawn after the health, and a dark red
  **shadow** of your health a moment ago: the gap shows how fast you are losing health

- **Icons that light up only in real danger:** healthstone, healing potion, bandage and your
  own defensive spells light up when your health is below the line **and the heals being cast
  on you will not lift it above**.
- **The line follows the fight:** 35 % while a mob is on you, 25 % while none is, 30 % for
  tanks (all three adjustable).
- **Roles from the group** (tank, healer, damage; solo counts as damage) or set by hand. Tanks:
  own defensives first, no Divine Shield / Divine Protection for paladin tanks. Healers:
  healthstone first, potion last.
- **Aggro warning:** in a group an icon when a mob is on you; tanks see when their target is
  not on them.
- **Bandage** only while no mob is on you and not within 60 s of your last one.
- **`/fhf hold`:** no lit icons until the end of the next fight.
- Ready icons can show dimmed; grey ones are not ready. Shown only in combat by default; solo,
  party and raid switches.
- Settings window (`/fhf config`, also in the game's AddOns settings) in the style of the game's
  own windows, like Full Mana Forever: the metal frame with the logo, gold labels, red buttons,
  values with arrows ◄ 35 % ►; icons in the frame of the game's action buttons.
- Shared and own profiles, 9 languages.

**Full Health Forever 0.0.1 - 0.0.4**
